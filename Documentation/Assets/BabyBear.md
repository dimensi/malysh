# Иконка «Малыш»

`BabyBear.generated.png` — оригинал с прозрачным фоном, созданный встроенным генератором изображений. Маска построена по альфа-каналу с порогом 128, обрезана по контуру и центрирована на квадрате. Potrace 1.16 преобразовал маску в редактируемый `BabyBear.svg` и PDF размером 25 × 25 pt. В iOS PDF используется как шаблон и получает системный цвет.

## Запрос генератору

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: custom native iOS tab bar symbol, not a home-screen app icon. Create ONE centered adorable baby teddy bear HEAD icon on a truly transparent background. It must read clearly as a little bear at 24 points: round head, two distinct round ears, tiny closed sleepy eyes, simple small oval muzzle and tiny nose. Calm, tender character suitable for a newborn-care diary. Crisp smooth geometric vector-friendly monochrome BLACK line art only, with consistent bold rounded strokes, roughly 7% of the head width. Large simple shapes and very few interior strokes; balanced symmetrical silhouette. No body, no lettering, no badge, no square background, no gradients, no shadows, no fur or texture, no mockup, no multiple variations. Transparent negative spaces. Square canvas with the complete symbol centered and 12% clear margin on each side. This raster will be traced into a template vector PDF for iOS to tint automatically.
