Malysh is a Russian-language care diary for parents. The main diary works locally on iPhone without an app account. An Apple Account with iCloud is only needed to test optional iCloud sync and family sharing. The app provides no medical recommendations and does not use HealthKit. Measurements are entered manually. If iCloud sync is enabled, the diary includes those measurements in its private or invite-only shared CloudKit storage.

1. Start with «Создать дневник» (Create a diary), enter a fictional name, then tap «Создать дневник» again. The «Сегодня» (Today) tab contains the care actions.
2. In «Сегодня», use «Кормление» (Feeding) to start a breastfeeding timer or log a bottle. Start «Сон» (Sleep) to see its timer. Use «Подгузник» (Diaper) to log a change. The «История» (History) tab shows saved events and the «Статистика» (Statistics) tab shows daily totals and a timeline.
3. Open «Малыш» (Child) to edit the profile, add measurements, view their charts or open the photo album. Photos can be imported using the system picker. The in-app camera requires camera access and asks for permission to save the original to Photos.
4. With an active breastfeeding or sleep timer, lock the iPhone to view the Live Activity. Dynamic Island display depends on the device. For feeding reminders, use «Настройки» → «Напоминания» and select an interval. Delivery depends on system settings and permissions.
5. For family sharing, on a device signed in to iCloud, open «Настройки» (Settings), enable «Синхронизация через iCloud», then choose «Пригласить второго родителя». Open the invitation on another iPhone signed in to a different Apple Account. Both phones can use the same diary after acceptance and sync. Use fictional records when testing.
6. Apple Watch companion: install Malysh on a paired iPhone and Apple Watch, open a diary on iPhone, then open the watch app. After the first sync, log a diaper or start a timer on the watch. Reconnect to the iPhone to transfer actions recorded offline. The iPhone app requires iOS 27.0 or later, and the watch app requires watchOS 26.0 or later.
7. PDF report (v1.13.0): open «Малыш» (Child) → «Отчёт для педиатра». Choose a period and short or detailed report. The PDF is generated on the iPhone from the selected diary. Save or share it using the system share sheet. No data is automatically sent to a doctor or to the developer.
